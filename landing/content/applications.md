## Entry fee

**💰 45 €** for your main category, **20 €** for each secondary category.

You can pick one main category and as many secondary ones as you like. Main applications are accepted first.

**📅 Tournament:** February 20–21, 2027

**📍 Location:** Novi Sad, Serbia (venue TBA)

## Categories

- Longsword: Open, Women's
- Sabre: Open, Women's
- Experimental: Longsword Fechtschule

There are 24 places in Open categories, 12 in Women's and Experimental.

Please give the [competition rules]({{< relref "/rules" >}}) a read before you apply.
