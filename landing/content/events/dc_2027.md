## See you in February!

Duke's Cup is coming back on **February 20–21, 2027** in Novi Sad, and this time it's a two-day event. We'll fence longsword and sabre, plus one more category that we're keeping secret for now.

Whether it's your first tournament or your tenth, come fence with us, see old friends and make new ones. We'd love to have you there.

## Key Information

**📅 Date:** February 20–21, 2027

**📍 Location:** Novi Sad, Serbia. We're still settling on the venue and will share it soon.

**📝 Applications:** open on **November 8, 2026** via Google Forms — [how to apply]({{< relref "/applications" >}})

**💰 Entry fee:** TBA
