## See you in February!

Duke's Cup is coming back on **February 20–21, 2027** in Novi Sad, and this time it's a two-day event. We'll fence longsword and sabre, plus an experimental category: Longsword Fechtschule.

Whether it's your first tournament or your tenth, come fence with us, see old friends and make new ones. We'd love to have you there.

## Key Information

**📅 Date:** February 20–21, 2027

**📍 Location:** Novi Sad, Serbia. We're still settling on the venue and will share it soon.

**📝 Applications:** open on **November 8, 2026** via [Google Forms](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScibBa4dlGq6S_sXucS6nCueWwHRBGQbz86LzAl7SpXhRBhrA/viewform?usp=header) — [how to apply]({{< relref "/applications" >}})

**💰 Entry fee:** 45 € for your main category, 20 € for each secondary category. You can pick one main category and as many secondary ones as you like. Main applications are accepted first.

## Categories

| Weapon | Divisions |
|--------|-----------|
| ⚔️ Longsword | Open, Women's |
| 🗡️ Sabre | Open, Women's |
| 🧪 Experimental | Longsword Fechtschule |
