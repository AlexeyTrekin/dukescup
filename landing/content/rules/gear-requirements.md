## Sabre

The sabre category is dedicated to late-19 century fencing, and the weapons should correspond to the historical examples. The sabre should have a bell guard or multi-branch guard and moderately curved or straight blade. Other configurations may not be allowed, in case of doubts contact us.

- Steel blade
- Full length up to 100 cm (in a straight line)
- Weight: 750-1000 gr
- Point of balance: 7-12 cm from the guard
- Blade flex 6-11 kg, measured with the [Franklin method](https://swordstem.com/2022/10/17/sword-flex/), without residual bend
- Recommended loop point; other types of point blunt should be additionally protected with duct tape, rubber or leather overlay
- The blade and guard should not have nicks, notches, barbs or other elements dangerous to the opponent or the gear.

## Longsword

**Sword:** federschwert or longsword blades are allowed, if the blade flex is within the limits below.

These requirements also apply to the experimental Longsword Fechtschule category.

- Steel blade
- Full length 120-135 cm
- Weight 1300-1700 grams
- Blade flex 10-16 kg, measured with the [Franklin method](https://swordstem.com/2022/10/17/sword-flex/)
- Rolled, thick or spatulated tip; rolled tip is recommended
- The schilt and the guard must not have sharp points
- The blade and guard should not have nicks, notches, barbs or other elements dangerous to the opponent or the gear.

## Mandatory protective equipment

Every fighter must use the following equipment

- Fencing mask, certified at Level 1 (350N) at least
- Mask overlay protecting the top of the mask and the back of the head and neck
- Protective jacket without gaps
- Rigid breast protection under the jacket
- Rigid groin protection (for men)
- Protection for throat and neck (a separate gorget, or a similar protection built in the jacket), blade catcher is highly recommended
- Rigid elbow and forearm protection
- Rigid knee and shin protection
- Fencing gloves for both hands, suitable for the weapon
  - Longsword: longsword gauntlets or gloves; full-plastic gauntlets like SPES, Kvetun etc. are recommended. Soft gloves like Red Dragons or hardened leather gloves are not allowed.

**NB! Open metal elements of protection, except for the mask, are forbidden!**
