## Admission and requirements

You need to be 18 or older to take part.

Women's categories are for women only. Open categories are for everyone.

**Registration and fee.** Register for the event and pay the participation fee: 45 € for your primary category and 20 € for each additional one.

**Primary and secondary categories.** When you register, pick one category as your primary; any others you choose are secondary. We accept primary applications first. If you apply for both an Open and a Women's category, the Women's one is your primary and the Open one is secondary.

By applying, you confirm that you have read and understood these rules and agree to follow them.

## Categories

- Longsword: Open.
- Longsword: Women.
- Sabre: Open.
- Sabre: Women.
- Experimental: Longsword Fechtschule.

Open categories accept 9 to 24 participants, Women's categories accept 6 to 12 participants.

Women's categories will be conducted if the number of participants is 6 or more, otherwise the applications will be moved to Open category, with possibility to withdraw the application.
