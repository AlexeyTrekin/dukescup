# Tournament rules

## Overview
These rules cover:
- tournament structure
- fight rules
- code of conduct and [penalties](#penalties)

The experimental Longsword Fechtschule category has [its own rules]({{< relref "experimental-longsword-fechtschule.md" >}}).

## 1. Competition rules

### 1.1 Tournament structure

Each category consists of three stages: the group stage (qualification), the playoff and the finals. The rules of fights are the same at all the stages.

#### 1.1.1 Category size

| Category | Participants | Group stage | Playoff |
|----------|:------------:|-------------|---------|
| Open | 9-16 | groups of 5-6 | top 8 |
| Open | 17-24 | groups of 5-6 | top 16 |
| Women's | 6-7 | one group | top 4 (semifinals) |
| Women's | 8-12 | two groups | top 8 |

If the number of participants can't be split into groups of 5-6, some groups will have 4 fighters.

#### 1.1.2 The group stage

1. Every fighter fights every other fighter of the group once.
2. The groups are seeded by the organizers with respect to the clubs specified in the applications and the [HEMA Ratings](https://hemaratings.com/) of the fighters, if applicable. Fighters of the same club are put into different groups. If you don't want to be in one group with someone, specify the same club in your applications.
3. After the group stage, the fighters of all the groups are rated by the following criteria, in this order:
   1. Points gained: the sum of all the points the fighter scored in all the exchanges;
   2. Number of doubles (fewer is better);
   3. Points lost: the sum of all the points the opponents scored against the fighter (fewer is better).

   As the groups may have different size, all the criteria are normalized to the number of fights: the sum is divided by the number of fights the fighter had.
4. The top fighters of the rating proceed to the playoff (see [1.1.1](#111-category-size)).

#### 1.1.3 The playoff and the finals

1. The playoff is a single-elimination bracket: the winner of each fight proceeds to the next round, the loser leaves the tournament.
2. The bracket is seeded by the rating after the group stage: 1st against 16th, 2nd against 15th, and so on (1st against 8th in the top 8). The bracket is arranged so that the top 4 fighters can meet each other only in the semifinals.
3. The winners of the semifinals fight for gold, the losers of the semifinals fight for bronze.
4. Draws are not allowed in the playoff and the finals. If the score is equal at the end of the fight, an additional round of 1 exchange and 30 seconds at most is conducted: the fighter who scores in this exchange wins. If the winner is not assigned then, we conduct an additional round with a 0.5 points handicap assigned by a coin flip.

#### 1.1.4 Technical loss and withdrawal

1. In the group stage, a technical loss (black card, or failing to come to the fight) affects only this fight: it is recorded as 7-0 in favour of the opponent.
2. If a fighter withdraws from the tournament or is disqualified during the group stage, all their fights in the group are cancelled and are not counted for anyone.
3. In the playoff and the finals, the opponent of the fighter who got a technical loss or withdrew proceeds to the next round.

### 1.2 The rules of fights

1. A fight consists of 7 exchanges and lasts 2 minutes of active fighting at most: the timer stops whenever the fight is stopped.
2. An exchange is any effective action: a hit that gives points, a double or a blow-afterblow. A red card, weapon loss, ring out or a fall also end the exchange. A yellow card does not.
3. A stop without a decision (no exchange, unclear exchange, a stop for safety reasons) is not counted as an exchange.
4. The fight ends after the 7th exchange or when the time is over. The fighter with more points wins.

**NB! This means that you need to take the maximum from every exchange: each zero-points exchange means you have lost your chance to get rating.**

### 1.3 Longsword

1. Cuts and thrusts to the head (including neck) or body give 3 points; to the limbs (including hands), 1 point.
2. Double: no points to both fighters.
3. Blow-afterblow: no points to both fighters, except for the hits to the head and neck: there is no afterblow after a hit to the head or neck, so this hit gets its points.
4. A one-handed strike gives 1 point, if it is clean. If a one-handed strike makes a double or blow-afterblow with a two-handed strike, it is not counted at all, and the opponent gets the points for their strike as for a clean hit. A double or blow-afterblow of two one-handed strikes gives no points.
5. Weapon loss gives 3 points to the opponent.
6. Ring out gives 1 point to the opponent.

### 1.4 Sabre

Sabre is judged by the priority (right of way) rules. The priority is assigned per Alfred Hutton's rules: the attack is the lunge.

1. A hit to any valid zone gives 1 point.
2. Weapon loss or ring out gives 1 point to the opponent.
3. No afterblow: a hit made after the opponent's hit gets no points.
4. In case of a double:
   - if one hit is to the leg and the other one is not, the hit to the higher zone gets 1 point, regardless of the priority;
   - attack against counterattack: the fighter who initiated the attack gets the point;
   - parry-riposte against redoubling: the riposte gets the point;
   - if the judges can't assign the priority clearly, it is a double, no points. The same goes when the tempo is on the edge between a double and a later hit, and the first hit was made without priority (a counterattack with a slight tempo advantage).

### 1.5 General rules for all weapons

1. Any cuts performed by the back of the sabre or the flat of any blade give no points;
2. Any qualified slices give 1 point;
3. Allowed special techniques:
   - Grabbing the opponent's arms, body or sword hilt, or the strong part of their blade if it's static, without arm locks or throws, does not grant points. Wrestling is stopped after 3 to 5 seconds, or if dangerous situation occurs;
   - Demonstration of pommel strikes to the mesh of the mask gives 1 point, stopping action;
4. Forbidden zones: back of the head, spine, groin, back of the knees, feet;
5. Explicitly forbidden actions: strikes with the sword guard, punches and kicks, joint locks, throws;
6. Falling gives 1 point to the opponent except situations when the falling is a part of technical action.
7. If two actions happen at the same time (i.e. losing the weapon and getting strike to the head) the HIGHER score is applied;
8. Ring out is unintentional leaving the fighting area with both legs.

## 2. Rights and duties of fighters and seconds

The fighter can have a second (cornerman), who can advise the fighter between bouts and substitute the fighter in filing the challenges. Misbehavior of the second can be judged as of the fighter himself. 

### 2.1 The fighter has right to:

- Ask the referee to repeat the fencing bout description
- Acknowledge a valid attack to himself, or discard an attack to his opponent.
  - This is not counted as a challenge
  - The referee has the final word on the exchange result, but should take this acknowledgement into consideration
- Challenge on the referee's decision or points assignment
  - The challenge should be called right after the decision
  - The challenge is addressed to the head referee of the area, specifying the reason. Then the judges gather to discuss the challenge and make a new decision

### 2.2 Allowed reasons for the challenges are:

- Mistake in the fencing phrase analysis
- Mistake in qualification of the attacking or defensive actions
- Mistake in recording of the results by secretary

Other reasons of the challenges are not valid and can't be called

### 2.3 Fighter obligations

- By submitting the application to the tournament, the fighters confirm that they have read, understood, and agree to respect the tournament rules
- The fighters should arrive at the tournament venue not later than the registration for their category ends. We can't guarantee participation of the late fighters.
- The fighter should prepare for the fight in advance. The fighters are given 60 seconds to enter the area after the fight announcement. If a fighter fails to do so, the fight is transferred 2 fights forward. On the second failure, the present fighter is awarded technical victory (see [1.1.4](#114-technical-loss-and-withdrawal)).
- After the fight, fighters should arrive at the secretary to confirm that the results are recorded correctly.

## 3. Refereeing regulations

The referee crew of an area consists of:
- the referee (head judge) of the area
- corner (assisting) judge 
- secretary of the area
- video replay operator (optional)

### 3.1 Referee crew duties

- The referee calls the participants to the area and checks their equipment
- The referee leads the conduct of the match with voice commands and gestures.

**Typical commands:**

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Come to the area - "Name 1": red, "Name 2": blue | invitation to the fight |
| Next fight - "Name 1", "Name 2" | signal for preparation to the next fight |
| Salute! | signal for the fighters to greet each other, judges and spectators |
| Ready? | request for confirmation |
| Fight!/Fence! | signal for the beginning of a bout. The timer is started at the signal |
| Stop! | signal to stop the fight. The fighter is interrupted at the signal |

After the "stop" command, the referee describes the fencing phrase. After the description, the decision is announced:
- Red 1 (3) points, Blue 1 (3) points
- Double, no points
- No exchange (means that there were no qualified actions before stop command)
- Unclear exchange, no decision (means that judges can't reliably call the result and thus discard this exchange)
- "Final score <red: X, blue: Y>, winner <red/blue>" (or "draw" in the group stage) - signal for the finish of the match and the result.

Only the referee gives vocal commands at the area, except for the safety reasons.

### 3.2 Corner judges

Corner judges follow the actions of both fighters, including successful attacks, tempo and the violations.

### 3.3 Secretary duties

The secretary of the area:
- Uses a laptop or a phone with HEMAGON to measure time and keep the protocol; 
- All the changes are made by the command of the referee, and only after the command;
- Announces for the referee 15 seconds before the time end;
- Calls the names of the fighters for the next fight.

### 3.4 Regulations for the judges' council and decision

The referee gathers the side judge and (optional the fencers) and asks the questions about the fencing phrase description, the judges answer only "yes", "no" or "refrain/ no opinion".

The question may consider:
- tempo (lead) of one of the attacks
- priority (right of way) of one of the actions (sabre)
- qualification of one of the attacks
- sector of the attack

And should be asked by the form:
1. Call the direction of the action ("From red fencer…")
2. Call the action ("cut/thrust/parry")
3. Call the questionable matter ("Is it qualified?" , "Is the target valid?")

According to the answers, the referee decides the results.

## 4. Penalties {#penalties}

There are 4 levels of the penalties

* **Warning** 🟨, (yellow card), is given for an accidental or mild misconduct, is recorded in the protocol by the secretary, and does not affect the results;  
* **Penalty point** 🟥, (red card), is given for the second or subsequent case of mild misconduct, or a severe misconduct, and gives 1 point to the opponent; Also, the actions of the carded fighter in the exchange are discarded.
* **Technical loss** ⬛, (black card) is given for critical misconduct or multiple lesser violations, and leads to the loss in the fight (see [1.1.4](#114-technical-loss-and-withdrawal))
* **Disqualification from the tournament and the event** may be issued in case of severe and malicious misconduct by the joint decision of the event organizers after the meeting.

For the proper enforcement of this system all the penalties are cumulative, i.e. yellow and red cards remain in force until the event ends. The third red card or the second black card automatically mean a disqualification from the tournament.

Violations table:

| Violation | First time | Second time | Subsequent cases of the same violation |
| ----- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Protocol violation**<br>• No salute on arrival at the area<br>• No handshake after the end of the fight<br>• Leaving the area before the results were announced<br> • Intentional leaving the ground during the fight<br> • Arriving at the area without referee's command <br>• Attacking before "FIGHT" command or after "STOP" command  <br> • Not being present at the fighting area a minute after being called <br>• Arguing with the judges outside of regulated challenge form  | 🟨 | 🟨 | 🟥 |
| **Self-endangerment**<br>• Exposing the forbidden areas to the opponent<br>• Exposing the back to the opponent<br> • Fighting with inadequate or damaged gear<br>• Taking the gear elements off before STOP command<br> | 🟨 | 🟨 | 🟥 |
| **Endangering opponent**<br>• Attacking of the forbidden areas<br>• Excessive force use and/or bad weapons control <br>• Striking with anything but the weapon blade <br>• Wrestling except for specifically allowed <br>• Throwing weapon <br>• Other actions threatening safety, on referee's decision. | 🟨 | 🟥 | 🟥 |
| **Severe misconduct**<br>• Refusal to follow the instructions of the referee <br>• Public swearing| 🟥 | 🟥 | ⬛ |
| **Bad sportsmanship**<br>• Refusal to begin/continue the assigned fight except for medical reasons<br>• Fixed match (intentional loss to promote other participant)  | ⬛ | ⬛ * | ⬛ * |
| **Violation of public behavior**<br>• Presence at the venue under alcoholic or drug influence<br>• Criminal offence | ⬛ * | ⬛ * | ⬛ * |

* can lead to immediate disqualification from the event

In case of intentional or severe violation, it can be penalized more severely than listed in the table, by the decision of the head referee of the tournament.

Any other violations which are not explicitly listed here can be addressed and penalized by the referee of the area with respect to the spirit of the rules and good sportsmanship.


## Injuries and withdrawal from the tournament {#injuries-and-withdrawal-from-the-tournament}

In case of injury, the organizers can give the fencer a break of maximum 10 minutes for medical help and recovery. If after this period the participant is unable to continue the fights, the head referee of the event decides the withdrawal from the competition.

The medical crew of the tournament has the right to suspend the participants from the tournament in case of risk for their health, even if the participants want to continue fighting.
