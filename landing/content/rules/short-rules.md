The tournament in one minute: what's special and what's different from other tournaments. For the details, see the full [tournament rules]({{< relref "tournament-rules.md" >}}) and [gear requirements]({{< relref "gear-requirements.md" >}}).

## What makes it different

- **Every exchange counts.** We rate fighters by the points they score, not by wins. An exchange with no points for you is a lost chance, even in a fight you win.
- **7 exchanges per fight**, 2 minutes of active fighting at most. There is no score limit that ends the fight early.
- **Doubles give no points** to either fighter.
- **Sabre with priority** following Alfred Hutton: the attack is the lunge.
- **Experimental category: Longsword Fechtschule**, with its own rules.

## Tournament system

Qualification in groups of 4-6 fighters, then the playoff. The rating is shared across all groups; the first criterion is the total points scored, not wins and losses.

## Longsword

- Head, neck and body: 3 points. Arms, hands and legs: 1 point.
- Double or blow-afterblow: no points. But there is no afterblow after a hit to the head or neck: it scores 3.
- One-handed strike: 1 point. In a double or afterblow against a two-handed strike, only the two-handed one scores.
- Weapon loss: 3 points to the opponent. Ring out: 1 point.

## Sabre

- Any valid hit: 1 point. No afterblow.
- In a double, priority decides: the attack beats the counterattack, the riposte beats the redoubling. A hit above the legs beats a hit to the legs. Unclear priority: no points.
- Weapon loss or ring out: 1 point to the opponent.
